**Note on Adjustments Made:**

The original draft had a strong foundation, with compelling character arcs and a well-structured plot. My adjustments focused on enhancing its cinematic potential by:

1.  **Tightening Pacing:** Streamlined action sequences and dialogue exchanges. Removed redundant descriptions, allowing the visuals and character reactions to carry more weight. This ensures a snappier, more engaging read.
2.  **Elevating Dialogue:** Refined lines to be more concise, character-specific, and impactful. For instance, Kaito's cynicism is sharper, Thorne's conviction more chilling, and Hana's desperation more immediate. Parentheticals were used sparingly, only when absolutely necessary to convey nuance not apparent from the line itself or the preceding action.
3.  **Heightening Tension:** Emphasized visceral details in action scenes and Kaito's internal struggles. The memory flashes were made more abrupt and disorienting, increasing their psychological impact. The reveal of Thorne's true nature and Kaito's connection to him were amplified for maximum emotional punch.
4.  **Visual Storytelling:** Enhanced action descriptions to be more vivid and cinematic, guiding the reader (and ultimately, the camera) to focus on key images, sounds, and emotional beats. For example, Kaito's apartment evolution, the Pan-Memory vault's chaotic beauty, and the immediate impact of the "truth bomb."
5.  **Standard Formatting Compliance:** Ensured all elements adhere strictly to industry-standard screenplay formatting, from scene headings to character names and dialogue blocks, making it immediately readable for production teams.

The goal was to create a script that not only tells a powerful story but *feels* like a movie on the page, ready for development.

## MNEMONIC ECHOES

### ACT I

**SCENE 1**

INT. KAITO'S APARTMENT - NIGHT

The air HUMS with a dozen high-tech processors. Beyond the grime-streaked window, NEO-TOKYO shimmers – a vertical landscape of neon and holographic advertisements.

KAITO "GHOST" AKIRA (30s, lean, sharp eyes) is hunched over his console. The intense blue glow of his AUGMENTED REALITY GLASSES illuminates his face, data streams dancing across the lenses. Empty instant ramen cups, a tangle of wires, and bespoke hardware clutter his desk.

A holographic PROJECTION of a pixelated, glitching data stream flickers beside him – his only companion.

Kaito’s fingers fly across a custom neuro-interface keyboard. On his main screen, a complex digital maze unravels, layers of encryption peeling back.

Across from him, a nervous YOUNG MAN (20s) fidgets in a cheap suit.

<center>YOUNG MAN</center>
> Just... the recipes. Family legacy. They were… gone.

Kaito grunts, eyes locked on the screen. His fingers tap a final sequence.

A bright GREEN "SUCCESS" icon flashes. Data flows into a small, encrypted drive.

<center>KAITO</center>
> Next time, back up your damn legacy. Payment first.

The Young Man fumbles for credits, relieved. Kaito pockets them with practiced ease, his focus already returning to the swirling data. He pulls off his glasses, rubbing his temples.

For a fleeting second, the city lights blur. A fragmented image flashes in his mind: a flickering RED LIGHT. The distant WAIL of a siren. A child's desperate, unheard SCREAM.

Kaito blinks. The image gone, replaced by the persistent hum of his machines. He sighs, replacing his glasses. The world snaps back into sharp, digital focus.

**SCENE 2**

INT. KAITO'S APARTMENT - DAY

Later. The ramen cups are gone, replaced by another pile. Kaito is mid-system scan when his secure comm unit CHIMES. An unscheduled visitor. He scowls.

Through the smoked glass of his door, he sees HANA SATO (20s, sharp, determined, but deeply distressed). Her eyes are red, her expensive synthetic silk coat rumpled. This isn’t a routine job.

Kaito hesitates, then hits the unlock.

Hana steps in, taking in the chaotic space. Her gaze lands on the holographic data stream, then on Kaito.

<center>HANA</center>
> Kaito Akira? They call you "Ghost."

<center>KAITO</center>
> Some do. Others just call me "expensive." What do you want?

<center>HANA</center>
> My husband. Kenji. He’s... hollow.

Kaito raises an eyebrow, leaning back in his chair.

<center>KAITO</center>
> Dead? Missing? I don’t find people. I find data.

<center>HANA</center>
> His memories. Stolen. Not deleted, but… erased. Rewritten. Our wedding day. Our first anniversary. Our fights. The good, the bad… everything that made *us*.

Her voice cracks. She pulls out a small, portable data reader. The screen shows a holographic image of a smiling, intelligent man – KENJI. Below it, a neural scan, riddled with dark, empty chasms.

<center>HANA</center>
> He’s a shell. The police called it a data hack. A privacy breach. They don’t understand. This wasn't theft. It was an amputation.

Kaito takes the reader, his brow furrowing as he examines the neural scan. The precision is unnerving.

<center>KAITO</center>
> This isn't black market. This is high-level. Dangerous.

<center>HANA</center>
> I know. But you’re the only one. Kenji was a neuro-engineer. Specialized in mnemonic interfaces. This was personal. Someone *knew* him.

Kaito shoves the reader back.

<center>KAITO</center>
> Too high-profile. Too much risk. I’m out.

Hana’s eyes flash, her grief turning to steel.

<center>HANA</center>
> My husband is still *alive*. He just doesn't remember *why*. If you won't help, no one will. What price do you put on a soul?

Kaito’s gaze drifts to his own holographic data stream, then back to the fractured neural scan. The meticulous erasure. The chilling sophistication. It’s a puzzle. And Hana's raw desperation… it stirs that flicker of red light in his own mind.

<center>KAITO</center>
> (Reluctantly)
> Details. Everything. Don't hold back.

Hana lets out a shuddering breath, a glimmer of hope in her eyes.

**SCENE 3**

INT. KAITO'S APARTMENT - NIGHT

Kaito’s screens display Kenji’s entire digital footprint – work logs, personal files, social media fragments. Immense data, but specific gaps stand out like craters.

He runs advanced forensic tools. His AR glasses project intricate web diagrams, highlighting anomalies.

<center>KAITO</center>
> (Muttering to himself)
> Not just wiped. Sculpted. An artist.

He finds traces of an advanced, proprietary memory extraction protocol. Far beyond anything on the darknet. The thief isn't just taking. They're *refining*.

The holographic data stream beside him pulses brighter, glitching more intensely. Another flash in Kaito's mind: a CHILD'S WHIMPER, barely audible over a ROARING SOUND. He shakes his head, focusing.

**SCENE 4**

INT. SHADY DATA DEN - NIGHT

NEO-TOKYO's underbelly. A cramped, smoke-filled room beneath a bustling market. Cables snake along the walls, connecting dozens of mismatched screens. The air HUMS with illicit data.

CACHE (40s, jovial, paranoid, constantly twitching) is a living node in the black market's nervous system. His eyes dart around. He sits amidst a fort of old servers.

Kaito, looking out of place, stands patiently.

<center>KAITO</center>
> "Neuro-Weaver." Advanced memory manipulation. Leaves victims... empty. Info.

Cache's eyes narrow. He runs a nervous hand through his greasy hair.

<center>CACHE</center>
> Woah, Ghost. Heavy stuff. Street talk. Myth. Doesn't just copy. *Relocates*. Leaves a gap. Or worse... fills it.

<center>KAITO</center>
> Concrete. IP addresses? Device signatures?

<center>CACHE</center>
> Concrete? Nah. Whispers, though… about an old hand. Disgraced neuro-scientist. THORNE. Aris Thorne. Went dark years ago. Rumor was he tinkered with something like that. Got too ambitious. Erased himself, they said.

Kaito processes the name. It means nothing.

<center>CACHE</center>
> (Leaning in)
> They also say... victims sometimes get a *gift*. A signature. Like an artist.

Kaito nods slowly. An artist. That aligns.

<center>KAITO</center>
> Any trace of Thorne's operations? Old labs?

Cache shakes his head, genuinely spooked.

<center>CACHE</center>
> Gone. Wiped. Cleaner than a new neural implant. But... (he pulls up a fuzzy image on a small screen) ...some whispers point to unusual server activity. Deep net. Highly encrypted. Corporate-level defense, but not attached to any known corp. Ghost in the machine, Ghost. That's all I got.

Kaito looks at the map coordinates. An abandoned industrial district. Too clean, too isolated.

**SCENE 5**

EXT. ABANDONED INDUSTRIAL DISTRICT - NIGHT

Kaito stands amidst decaying industrial hulks. The air is cold, haze clinging to skeletal factories. The coordinates lead him to a massive, derelict server farm, its windows boarded up.

**SCENE 6**

INT. ABANDONED SERVER FARM - NIGHT

Kaito slips through a broken utility access. Inside, the cavernous space is unexpectedly pristine. Rows upon rows of towering SERVER RACKS HUM with unseen power, indicator lights glowing like a digital city.

This isn’t abandoned. This is a meticulously maintained, *hidden* operation.

Kaito’s AR glasses project schematics, scanning for defenses. He finds a bespoke, highly advanced security system. He begins his infiltration, fingers dancing across a portable interface.

He bypasses layers of firewalls, each more complex than the last. He finds the unique digital signature he traced from Kenji’s stolen memories. It’s embedded here. And it's not alone.

On a large, central display unit, Kaito projects the sheer scale of the operation. Gigabytes upon gigabytes of *stolen memories*. Not raw data, but ANALYZED, CATALOGED, and cross-referenced fragments. Hundreds, perhaps thousands, of individuals. Kenji's wedding day memories are just a tiny drop in this ocean.

This isn't just about Kenji. This is about *everyone*.

Suddenly, a tripwire is triggered. RED ALARMS flash. Robotic SECURITY DRONES, sleek and silent, drop from the ceiling, optical sensors glowing.

Kaito curses, stuffing his equipment into his bag. He blasts a quick EMP burst from a wrist-mounted device, momentarily disabling the closest drones, buying precious seconds.

He sprints through the labyrinthine server aisles, dodging laser fire from re-engaging drones. He throws himself through the broken utility access, the door CLANGING shut behind him just as a burst of energy SLAMS into it.

Kaito stumbles outside, heart pounding. He looks back at the now-alight server farm. This isn't just a criminal organization. This is a powerful, ideological, and deeply dangerous force. The implications hit him like a physical blow. Identity. Memory. Humanity. All on the line.

He pulls out his comms.

<center>KAITO</center>
> (Into comms)
> Hana. We have a problem. A big one.

The glowing red alarms reflect in his AR glasses, a stark counterpoint to the city's neon.

### ACT II

**SCENE 7**

INT. KAITO'S APARTMENT - DAY

Kaito’s apartment is even more cluttered, his frustration palpable. His screens are filled with data on DR. ARIS THORNE: old academic papers, obscure patents, blurry corporate photos.

<center>KAITO</center>
> "Neuro-Curator." "Pan-Memory Initiative." This isn't a theory, it's a manifesto.

He cross-references Thorne's known projects with the advanced memory extraction protocols he found. It's a perfect match.

His comm unit chimes. It's Hana.

<center>KAITO</center>
> (Into comms)
> Thorne. His name is Aris Thorne. He's alive. And he's behind this. Kenji ever mention him?

A beat of silence from Hana. Then, hesitant.

<center>HANA (O.S.)</center>
> Kenji... worked with Thorne. Years ago. Before we met. A project... "therapeutic memory modification." For trauma victims.

<center>KAITO</center>
> Therapeutic? He's stealing people's entire pasts.

<center>HANA (O.S.)</center>
> Thorne’s vision... it radicalized. Kenji tried to pull out. He said Thorne believed suffering was a defect. Wanted to remove it. Create a... 'perfect' human experience.

Hana's voice is thick with guilt.

<center>HANA (O.S.)</center>
> I knew some of this. Thought he was gone. I should have told you.

<center>KAITO</center>
> (Voice hard)
> Sorry won't get Kenji's memories back. Any other former colleagues? Anyone who'd know more about Thorne's current whereabouts?

<center>HANA (O.S.)</center>
> Dr. Mei Lin. Kenji's mentor. She went dark after Thorne disappeared.

**SCENE 8**

INT. DR. MEI LIN'S LAB - NIGHT

A sterile, corporate research lab, humming with high-end bio-engineering equipment. DR. MEI LIN (50s, brilliant, haunted eyes) looks exhausted, clutching a coffee cup.

Kaito stands opposite her.

<center>MEI LIN</center>
> Thorne… I thought he was dead. Or locked away. His ideas… they were seductive, Kaito. Imagine a world without grief. Without rage. Without prejudice. A clean slate.

<center>KAITO</center>
> He’s not giving people a clean slate. He’s writing their novel for them. And for himself.

<center>MEI LIN</center>
> (Voice trembling)
> He calls it the "Pan-Memory." A centralized, curated archive of all human experience. He believes by controlling the past, he controls the future. He witnessed his own family's memories being forcibly altered during the ’42 purges. It broke him. Twisted his genius into this… monstrous idealism.

She pushes a datapad towards Kaito. It displays an intricate, terrifying blueprint for a vast, interconnected neural network.

<center>MEI LIN</center>
> This is what he showed us. His vision. To him, we're just data. Flawed, chaotic data that needs optimizing. He's not stealing memories for profit, Kaito. He's stealing souls. To build his goddamn utopia.

Kaito stares at the blueprint, a chill running down his spine.

<center>KAITO</center>
> Where is he operating?

Mei Lin shakes her head.

<center>MEI LIN</center>
> Too smart. Too careful. But he'll need to test his system. A smaller, contained network. A prototype.

**SCENE 9**

INT. PROTOTYPE MEMORY VAULT - NIGHT

Kaito moves like a ghost through a dimly lit, high-tech chamber. Mei Lin's lead – a hidden "test chamber" beneath an abandoned corporate tower.

In the center, transparent RECLINING PODS house unconscious subjects, tubes and wires connecting them to a central, glowing console. Projected onto the inner surface of one pod is a repeating loop of a serene, idealized memory: a young couple laughing on a pristine beach, the sun always setting perfectly.

Kaito feels cold dread. This isn’t rehabilitation. This is imprisonment.

He accesses the central console, his AR glasses decoding the data streams. Kenji's memory fragments are here, too. Being "scrubbed," "optimized," polished to an impossible, painful perfection.

Kaito brushes a hand against a pulsating data conduit, attempting a quick data siphon. The conduit SPARKs violently, OVERLOADING.

A blinding flash. Kaito recoils, crying out.

He's no longer in the vault. He’s back in the **FRAGMENTED MEMORY**: the ROARING FIRE. The SIREN. A CHILD'S SCREAM, closer now. His own. The SMELL of smoke and burning plastic. The feel of a SMALL HAND slipping from his.

Then, just as quickly, it’s gone. Kaito is back in the vault, gasping, shaking. He clutches his head. The experience was visceral, overwhelming. He can taste the smoke, feel the heat.

His own suppressed trauma. It wasn't just a malfunctioning memory chip. It was real. And the intensity... the raw, unfiltered truth of it. It feels connected. To Thorne. To his methods. Was he a victim too? An early test? The thought is horrifying.

Kaito stares at his trembling hands. The stakes just became profoundly personal.

**SCENE 10**

INT. KAITO'S APARTMENT - DAY

Kaito works feverishly, paranoia etched on his face. His screens are a blur of counter-measure programming. He’s building a "memory firewall," an anti-Thorne program.

He keeps glancing at his comms. Thorne knows he’s here. The hunt is on.

He receives a panicked call from Cache.

<center>CACHE (O.S.)</center>
> Ghost! They’re everywhere! Corporate goons. Looking for you. Not just digital anymore. They’re physical. You gotta move!

Kaito disconnects, grabs his essential gear. A flicker of movement at his window. A DRONE.

He smashes his comm unit, wipes his active drives, and bursts out the fire escape just as his door is BLASTED inward.

**SCENE 11**

EXT. NEO-TOKYO ROOFTOPS - NIGHT

Kaito leaps across rooftops, the city's neon reflecting off his slick coat. Below, Thorne's ENFORCERS, heavily armed, swarm the streets. Searchlights sweep the sky.

He's a ghost no longer. He's hunted.

He narrowly evades a patrol drone, ducking into a ventilation shaft. He navigates the city's hidden passages, relying on Cache's network for safe houses.

**SCENE 12**

INT. ABANDONED SUBWAY TUNNEL - NIGHT

One of Cache's safe houses. Grimy, but secure. Kaito works on his memory firewall, code swirling around him in AR. He’s fueled by cold logic, but tormented by the vivid flashes of his past. The fire. The hand. His family. It’s all coming back.

His new encrypted comm unit CHIMES. It’s Hana.

<center>HANA (O.S.)</center>
> Kaito, I found something. Kenji… so meticulous. He built a fail-safe. If Thorne ever... went too far.

<center>KAITO</center>
> What is it?

<center>HANA (O.S.)</center>
> A data key. Hidden in our old apartment. A back door to a legacy encryption protocol in Thorne's original network. Crude, but it might be the vulnerability you need.

A surge of relief, then renewed determination.

<center>KAITO</center>
> Send me the location. Stay safe. Thorne won't stop.

Kaito sees the map of Thorne's core facility on his screen, overlaid with old power station schematics. A sprawling, subterranean data vault. A fortress.

His vision blurs. The fire. The hand. He steadies himself, forcing the images back. Not now. Not yet. Focus.

**SCENE 13**

EXT. ABANDONED POWER STATION - NIGHT

The monolithic structure looms against the polluted Neo-Tokyo sky. Crumbling concrete, exposed rebar. But Kaito sees the tell-tale hum of hidden energy beneath. This is it. Thorne's sanctuary. His temple of controlled memory.

Kaito, armed with Hana's key, moves to infiltrate.

**SCENE 14**

INT. THORNE'S MAIN FACILITY - SUBLEVEL - NIGHT

Kaito moves through dark, subterranean corridors. He uses Hana's data key. An ancient, rusted access panel GRINDS open. He enters a new section – sleek, modern, menacing.

He feels a shift in the digital air. The network is alive, pulsing. He’s almost there.

Suddenly, the lights FLICKER on, blinding him. The corridor is filled with Thorne's SECURITY FORCES, heavily armed. Kaito is surrounded.

<center>THORNE (O.S.)</center>
> Welcome, Mr. Akira. I’ve been expecting you.

A man steps forward. DR. ARIS THORNE. Impeccably dressed, silver hair, calm, almost serene. But his eyes hold the terrifying conviction of a zealot.

Kaito’s equipment is instantly disarmed. His memory firewall program, so painstakingly crafted, is siphoned away.

<center>THORNE</center>
> You are persistent. A genuine anomaly. But you cannot fight the tide of progress.

Thorne gestures to a nearby monitor. A global broadcast timer is counting down.

<center>THORNE</center>
> The Pan-Memory will launch in moments. Humanity will finally be free. Free from the burden of their messy, subjective, painful pasts. A true utopia.

Kaito glares, defiant.

<center>KAITO</center>
> You're not freeing them. You're lobotomizing them. Turning them into puppets.

Thorne smiles, a chilling, pitying expression.

<center>THORNE</center>
> You misunderstand, Kaito. You, of all people, should understand the allure of a clean slate.

Thorne taps a control on his wrist. Kaito’s vision is suddenly overwhelmed. A torrent of images floods his mind – the fire, the sirens, the screams, the loss. But this time, it’s not fragmented. It’s COMPLETE. Raw. Unfiltered. He sees his family’s faces, distorted by terror. Feels the heat, the panic, the crushing grief. The small hand slipping from his. His child-self, paralyzed, watching it all burn.

Kaito collapses, gasping, tears streaming down his face. The memory is too much. Too real.

<center>THORNE</center>
> (Softly)
> An unfortunate incident, Kaito. An unstable mnemonic device, in the hands of an inexperienced operator. One of my earliest prototypes. You were merely a child, caught in the periphery. Your nascent brain fragmented the trauma as a defense mechanism. I saw it, even then, as proof of memory's inherent fragility. Its propensity to cause pain.

Thorne kneels beside Kaito, offering a hand.

<center>THORNE</center>
> Imagine. All that pain. Gone. Erased. Join us, Kaito. Let me integrate you. Release you from this burden. Help me build a new world. A better world. Free from the suffering you’ve just re-experienced.

Kaito looks up, his eyes wide with horror, grief, and dawning comprehension. His sense of self is utterly broken. Thorne activates the final upload sequence on the main screen, the timer reaching zero.

### ACT III

**SCENE 15**

INT. THORNE'S MAIN FACILITY - HOLDING CELL - CONTINUOUS

Kaito is in a transparent, energy-shielded holding cell. He’s curled in a ball, reliving the full horror of his childhood trauma. The fire. The faces of his family. The helplessness. The crushing, unbearable grief.

He sobs, raw and primal. The pain is immense. But amidst the agony, a strange clarity begins to emerge. This pain… it’s *his*. It forged him. It’s the reason he became "Ghost." The reason he fights for others’ memories.

He remembers his parents' love before the fire. Their laughter. Their lessons. Those memories, intertwined with the pain, make him who he is. He closes his eyes, accepting the truth. The trauma doesn't define him, but it is *part* of him. And that’s okay.

A new kind of resolve hardens his expression. He will not let Thorne take this from anyone else.

**SCENE 16**

INT. THORNE'S MAIN FACILITY - MAINTENANCE SHAFT - CONTINUOUS

Hana, DR. MEI LIN, and a small, determined group of Kenji’s former colleagues navigate a grimy maintenance shaft. Hana carries a comm unit Kaito planted on her – a subtle tracker.

<center>HANA</center>
> (Whispering into comms)
> Kaito, can you hear me? We're in. Diversion inbound. Get ready.

From a distance, EXPLOSIONS ROCK the facility. ALARMS WAIL. The guards outside Kaito's cell are momentarily distracted.

The energy shield flickers. Kaito, now ready, spots a loose data port near the cell's base. He pulls out a hidden, miniaturized neuro-jack – a last resort, hidden in his boot. He plugs it in, frantically re-activating his counter-program. He modifies it, making it less about 'destroying' and more about 'reintroducing chaos' – the truth of human experience.

The energy shield around his cell fails. He kicks it open, grabbing the neuro-jack.

**SCENE 17**

INT. THORNE'S MAIN FACILITY - PAN-MEMORY VAULT - CONTINUOUS

Kaito bursts into the CORE of the Pan-Memory vault. It’s breathtaking and terrifying. A vast, crystalline data network pulses with light and energy. Holographic projections of billions of interwoven memories swirl, vibrant and serene – too serene. This is Thorne’s curated utopia, on the verge of overwriting humanity.

DR. ARIS THORNE, directly interfaced with the main console, bathed in its ethereal glow, manipulates the final upload. His eyes are glazed with fanaticism.

<center>THORNE</center>
> (Without turning)
> You escaped. A predictable, messy outcome. Just like your memories, Kaito. Prone to chaos.

<center>KAITO</center>
> Chaos is reality, Thorne. Your perfection is a lie.

Kaito plugs his neuro-jack into a key access port. He unleashes his enhanced counter-program – the "truth bomb."

The crystalline network shudders. The serene memory projections begin to GLITCH, distort, fracture.

Thorne ROARS, digitally and physically. He unleashes a torrent of digital attacks, streams of "perfect" data designed to overwhelm Kaito. Psychological assaults based on Kaito's fears.

<center>THORNE</center>
> You want chaos, Kaito? I'll show you chaos! Your family! Your loss! Your helplessness!

Kaito stands firm, the raw, unfiltered truth of his own trauma no longer breaking him, but strengthening his resolve. He weaves through the digital attacks, dodging physical SECURITY DRONES that descend from the ceiling. He targets key nodes in Thorne's network, injecting raw, uncurated data.

Images of unfiltered human experience explode across the vault: not just sorrow, but exhilarating joy. Not just hate, but boundless love. Moments of painful growth, of messy triumph. The truth of human experience, in all its chaotic glory.

The Pan-Memory network strains, groans, unable to process the contradictions. Cracks spiderweb across the crystalline structure.

Thorne, still interfaced, begins to convulse. The torrent of unfiltered human experience – the very chaos and pain he sought to eliminate – overwhelms him. His own carefully constructed, "perfect" memory of his past shatters. He sees his family’s suffering, unmitigated, untamed. The truth of their forced memory alteration, the injustice, the sheer pain of it, floods his mind.

He SCREAMS, a broken, defeated sound, as his utopian network unravels around him. He collapses, mentally broken, trapped in the very reality he sought to escape.

The Pan-Memory network dies with a final, shuddering CRACKLE, plunging the vault into a dim, emergency light.

**SCENE 18**

INT. THORNE'S MAIN FACILITY - RECOVERY ZONE - DAY

The aftermath. The facility is a mess of sparking wires and disabled drones. THORNE is unconscious, taken into custody.

Kaito, exhausted but resolute, monitors the memory restoration process. Most of the stolen memories are being reintegrated into their original neural pathways. Some are fragmented, too altered to be fully restored, but their existence is now affirmed.

Hana sits beside a still-disoriented Kenji. His eyes are vacant, but as Kaito completes a final memory restoration patch, a flicker of recognition.

<center>KENJI</center>
> Hana…

Hana weeps, clutching his hand. The wedding memories might be gone in their original form, but the *connection* is back. The truth.

<center>HANA</center>
> We'll rebuild, Kenji. Together.

Kaito watches, a quiet satisfaction settling over him. He helped them keep their truth.

**SCENE 19**

INT. KAITO'S APARTMENT - WEEKS LATER - DAY

The apartment is noticeably brighter. Clutter is still present, but organized. Some ramen cups remain, but also a freshly brewed cup of real coffee.

Kaito sits at his console. His AR glasses are on his desk, unused. He’s looking at an old, slightly faded FAMILY PORTRAIT on his physical desk – a photo of his parents, and a younger him, smiling. Now, when he looks at it, the memories aren't fractured. They’re whole. The pain is there, yes, but so is the love, the joy, the warmth. It's his truth.

His comm unit CHIMES. A new client. The profile is different. Not a simple data retrieval. This client isn't looking to forget. They're looking to understand. To restore the *truth* of a difficult memory, not just remove it.

Kaito smiles faintly. He’s no longer just a "ghost" navigating data streams. He's a guardian. A sentinel of memory.

He accepts the client.

FADE OUT.
